Foil privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

In short: Foil reads receipts, chats and bank alerts on your own computer. Nothing about your customers or your bank account is sent anywhere unless you see exactly what would go and click to approve it. Your books in the cloud hold only amounts, payment states and times, never names, phone numbers, addresses or chat text.

What Foil reads, and when

  • WhatsApp Web: only the chat you have open, only what is already on screen, and only when you click one of Foil's buttons. Foil never sends messages, opens other chats or scrolls for you. Replies it writes are placed in the message box for you to send yourself.
  • Gmail (optional): if you connect it, Foil reads bank alert emails with read-only access to find the credits that match your customers' receipts. It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Screenshots you add: such as a bank-app history screenshot, read on your computer.

What stays on your computer

Customers' names, phone numbers, addresses, chat messages, receipts, your bank credits, your stock and any notes you keep are stored in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on this computer. Where Chrome's built-in model is available, receipts, chats, orders and replies are also read and written on this computer by that model.

What is sent, and only after you approve it

  • Google's Gemini service, through Firebase AI Logic: used for voice notes, for some languages, for cloud answers to your questions, and for any step you have chosen to run in the cloud because this computer has no built-in model. Each time, Foil shows you exactly what will be sent, with customer names and numbers replaced by neutral labels wherever the task allows, and waits for your click.
  • Your books, in Cloud Firestore: one entry per order or money decision with its amount, payment state and times, under an anonymous account tied to this browser. It holds no names, phone numbers, addresses, account numbers or chat text.
  • Google Sheets and Calendar (optional): only when you choose to send your orders or a delivery run there, after you see and approve what will be written, to files in your own Google account.
  • Spoken answers (optional): if you choose one of Google's online voices in Settings, the words of each answer are spoken by Google's speech service. Local voices keep them on this computer.

What Foil never does

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or profiling.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No sending of WhatsApp messages or any automation of WhatsApp.
  • No use of your data for anything other than the features you click.

Foil's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

You can turn the cloud off in Settings at any time, disconnect Gmail and Google, download your records, and erase everything Foil stored by removing the extension or clearing its data in Chrome.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on Foil's GitHub page.
